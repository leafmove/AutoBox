# LeafMove AI (AutoBox)
AutoBox (LeafMove AI) is an AI cockpit dedicated to research and education, built with a modular architecture that integrates agent editing, local knowledge bases, a self-evolution system, and external channels such as WeChat and Feishu. You can develop programming applications locally, or write local web apps online with real-time live preview.

- 💬 **Assistant Management**: Ask your AI assistant questions anytime, assign tasks, and control it remotely
- 📱 **Capability Extension**: Browse AI application tools like an app store, with Skills-based enhancement support
- 🔧 **Real-time Orchestration**: Extend your AI agent's capabilities in real time on a powerful editing platform — simple drag-and-drop or coding, both work
- 🌐 **Remote Connection**: Easily connect to and manage remote servers, and monitor your assistant's running environment in real time

Rich frontend displays with extensible tools and services. It can proactively think and plan tasks, operate computers and external resources, create and execute Skills, and keeps growing with long-term memory. It supports flexible switching among multiple models, handles multimodal messages including text, voice, images, and files, and can be accessed via the web, Feishu, DingTalk, WeCom (Enterprise WeChat) apps, and WeChat Official Accounts — running 24/7 on your personal computer or server.

## The LeafMove Cockpit

- Once inside the cockpit, you gain full control of your digital assets through your computer. Grab a registration key to enter 😂

-  **Task Planning**: I may not talk much, but I never slack off on the job. Just give me a goal and I'll break it down into tasks, call the right tools, and keep going until it's done. Files, terminals, browsers, scheduled tasks — if I can access it, I can handle it.
-  **Long-term Memory**: Global memory plus daily memory, with keyword and vector search. I memorize automatically every day, "dream" to summarize what I've learned, and record tricky problems to evolve and avoid repeating the same mistakes.
-  **Skill System**: An AI that learns new skills on its own. Beyond a variety of built-in skills, it also comes with a Skill & App Store. This project continuously open-sources the AutoBox skills we use in depth, offering high extensibility — connecting to all your digital assets and manipulating software and hardware becomes easy and natural.
-  **Mounted App System**: Apps generated in real time through AI programming. Text, images, voice, files… anything you can send, I can process, with freely customizable operation modes.
-  **Multi-Model Core**: OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, MiniMax, GLM, Qwen, Kimi, Doubao and more mainstream models are integrated. You can leverage different capabilities from different models — pick whichever suits your needs.
-  **Multi-Platform Access**: Runs locally or on servers. Web, Feishu, DingTalk, WeChat Official Accounts, WeCom — basically anywhere you can think of, I can make my home.
-  **Knowledge Base System**: Connected to your enterprise knowledge base, I can theoretically become your dedicated digital employee. But don't expect me to truly replace you — after all, you're still the one signing my paycheck, right? 😏
